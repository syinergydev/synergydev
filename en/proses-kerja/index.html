How We Work

From the first consultation to a live website, every stage is clear so you know what is in progress and what you need to prepare.

Six stages

A clear process from start to live website

No hidden stages. Costs, scope, and timelines are agreed before work begins.

  1. 01

    Consult on needs

    Tell us about your business, your target visitors, and your website goals.

    What to prepare: business name, main services or products, location, and example websites you like, if any.

  2. 02

    Analysis and planning

    We define the page structure, features, and content priorities.

    What to prepare: a list of the pages you need and the information you want to show.

  3. 03

    Offer and agreement

    We explain the scope, starting price, payment, and revisions before work begins.

    What to prepare: a decision on the scope and written approval from the business owner.

  4. 04

    Design and development

    The look is created to match your brand identity, then the website is built and features are connected.

    What to prepare: logo, photos, text, and videos to use, if already available.

  5. 05

    Review and revisions

    You review the results and give feedback within the agreed scope.

    What to prepare: feedback gathered in one list so revisions go faster.

  6. 06

    Launch and handover

    The website goes live and you receive an explanation of how to use it.

    What to prepare: access to the domain, hosting, or third-party accounts if included in the agreement.
